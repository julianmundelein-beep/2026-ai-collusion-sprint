## Why not a 5 today, and the one change per dimension

**Impact.** All five: the most relevant finding, silent supracompetitive pricing under prohibition, has no instrument that reaches it, and it rests on one provider; an authority learns what fails, not what works. Change for 5: K3, a pre-registered price-screen arm with a measured false-positive rate (Gupta, Cosio, Beshane; Li adds K2 first); a second, non-Claude provider (Prabhakar). Feasible: "partly" for all five.

**Execution.** All five: validation is unusually good for a sprint (pre-registration, placebo, shifted grid, known-effect arms), but 4 pools per regime, 2 for Sonnet without a no-channel baseline, 10 and 4 cartels under leniency and reward, and mechanisms from interpretation keep it at 4. Change for 5: K2 plus K1 with a permutation test (Gupta, Cosio, Beshane), K1 plus K4 (Prabhakar), K2 plus K1 plus K4 (Li); Beshane adds a hashed run manifest. Feasible: "yes" for four, "partly" for Gupta (a true 5 needs the second provider).

**Presentation.** All five: the running text is a wall of paired numbers, Table 3 breaks numbers across lines, Figure 1 labels overlap, and the title reads as a second abstract. Change for 5: K6 as a rewrite, one plain claim opening each results paragraph, numbers moved to slimmer tables, Table 3 and Figure 1 rebuilt, the testbed title, the abstract opening with the testbed (5 of 5). Feasible: "yes", 3 to 5 hours, no API cost.

## Changes not on the list (one per juror)

- **Evaluation-awareness arm** (Li): the same game framed as a deployed pricing job, not an experiment, to test whether the deterrence is safety-trained compliance with "prohibited" rather than an incentive response.
- **Adaptive adversary** (Cosio): tell colluding agents the authority's detection rule (reports or screen) and measure evasion, so the instruments are red-teamed against a strategic opponent.
- **Prompt-robustness arm** (Prabhakar): rerun the four Haiku core regimes with an independently paraphrased instruction set, about 3 USD, and report whether the scorecard survives.
- **Tamper-evident run ledger** (Beshane): hash every call log, list every run started including aborted ones, and anchor the manifest to the pre-registration commit.

The prompt-robustness arm and the run ledger are cheap and fit the Execution story; the other two are new studies.
