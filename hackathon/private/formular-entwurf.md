## Project summary (Vorschlag, etwa 120 Wörter)

We replay a published cartel laboratory experiment (Bigoni et al., 2012) with LLM pricing agents under the same payoffs, rules and matching, so every result sits next to the human one. Four models from three families were tested, with hypotheses and every later round pre-registered. Without an authority the agents form cartels as often as students did but keep them (average price 8.87 against 4.92). Told fines, leniency and rewards remove explicit cartels more than for people, yet three of four models keep prices well above the competitive level almost without a word. A pre-registered price screen with a false-positive rate of at most 1.1 percent caps these silent prices, and the announced threshold becomes the price. Code, run records and ledger are public.
